Voice commands you can say
  • "Open camera" — makes sure the camera is running
  • "What's in front of me?" — describes the scene
  • "Read this" — reads visible text aloud
  • "What denomination is this note?" — Indian currency check
  • "Start / stop object detection" — continuous live detection
  • "Call Mom" or "Call emergency"
  • "Send message to Mom saying I'll be late"
  • "Where am I?" — speaks your current location
  • "Find my phone" — plays a sound + vibrates this device
  • "Emergency" or "help me" — alert + call emergency contact
  • Anything else — asked directly to the camera as an open question
